Profile

01 / ABOUT

I'm LINTANG, a graphic and UI designer with eight years of practice spanning brand identity, editorial layout, and product interfaces. I trained in print production before moving into digital — that background shows up in how I treat grids, spacing, and hierarchy as structural, not decorative.

My process starts with constraints: the medium, the audience, the message. From there I build systems — type scales, color logic, component rules — that a brand or product can grow into, rather than one-off pages that don't scale.

Recent work includes identity systems for two Series-A startups, an editorial redesign for an independent publication, and ongoing UI work for a fintech dashboard.
